The Great Alignment

February 28 Planetary Parade

A "planetary alignment" occurs when multiple planets gather closely on one side of the Sun at the same time. While they appear to form a line in Earth's night sky, they are actually spread out across millions of miles of 3D space.

The famous February 28 alignment features all 7 outer/inner planets visible in the sky. It is a rare visual clustering that will not repeat with this exact grouping until 2040.
