Before you begin

Answer honestly based on what feels true for you today. There are no right or wrong answers. This predictor focuses on your interests, learning preferences, and comfort level—not on judging your worth.

This is a guidance tool, not a final verdict. A low score in any stream does not mean “you cannot do it”; it may simply mean you need more exploration or support before deciding.

How to use this result wisely

  • Compare the subjects in your top 2 or 3 options, not just the top one.
  • Talk to a teacher, parent, sibling, or counsellor before finalizing your stream.
  • If you feel weak in some subjects, choose based on interest + support + effort, not fear.
  • Check the exact subject combinations available in your own school before deciding.
  • Remember: your stream is important, but it does not permanently limit your future.
